# Mechanical interface (for the enclosure)

This file gives the board data the enclosure design (`hardware/oak-panel/`) needs. Nothing in the
mechanical files was changed.

Machine-readable data is regenerated by `tools/gen_envelope.py` into
`exports/mechanical/envelope.json`, together with a STEP envelope,
`power-halo-board-envelope.step`. The envelope STEP contains:

- the board with its holes;
- one box per part, from its courtyard and a conservative height;
- `KEEPOUT-<ref>` solids for mating plugs.

Coordinates use KiCad's convention: origin at the top-left board corner seen from the component
side, +x right, +y down, millimetres.

## Outline and mounting

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Outline | rectangle 136.0 × 86.0 mm, square corners, 1.6 mm thick (`exports/power-halo-board-outline.dxf`/`.svg`) |
| Mounting holes | 4 × Ø3.2 mm (M3), unplated, no copper: (4, 4), (132, 4), (4, 82), (132, 82) |
| Keep-out around each hole | 7 × 7 mm square centred on the hole (no parts, no copper) |
| Components | top side only |
| Bottom side | through-hole leads protrude up to ~2.5 mm; use standoffs ≥ 6 mm |
| Max height above board top | 14 mm envelope (J1 Mini-Fit Jr). Next tallest: C2/C3 bulk capacitors 10.8 mm, VH connectors 11 mm envelope, JP601/J602 headers with a shunt or plug 11.5 mm |
| Mounting hardware | M3 screws with nylon or metal standoffs; the holes have no copper, so either is fine |

The keepout depth covers the plug, the latch release and the wire bend radius. Each keepout is
as wide as the connector courtyard. Leave room above each plug to press its latch. Check the latch side against the
manufacturer drawings.

## Things to see or reach in the enclosure

- Status LEDs:
  - Green: D2 (+24V) at (119, 37), D531 (+5V_AUX) at (27.7, 25), D602 (halo status) at
    (106.2, 81.5).
  - Red faults: D101 (62.5, 39), D201 (97.5, 39), D301 (36.5, 47), D401 (71.5, 47),
    D501 (29, 39), D601 (107.5, 52).
  - None is required for operation. A service window over the board is enough.
- Test points: TP1/TP2 (+24V/GND) near J1, TP101–TP401 (panel 5 V), TP501/TP502 (Pi 5 V,
  +5V_AUX), TP601 (halo 5 V). They are 1.5 mm pads for probe access with the enclosure open.
- JP601 and J602 need finger or plug access with the back open only.

## Airflow and spacing

- See [power-and-thermal.md](power-and-thermal.md#enclosure-ventilated-petg) for vent sizing:
  ≥ 100 cm² inlet low and ≥ 100 cm² outlet high.
- Keep ≥ 10 mm of air above the components and ≥ 5 mm between the converter modules or
  C2/C3 and any PETG wall.
- The converter modules are U101, U201, U301, U401 and U501 at (43, 32), (78, 32), (56, 54),
  (91, 54) and (9.5, 32), each 6.5 × 7.5 mm.

## Wi-Fi antenna keepout (Raspberry Pi 3B)

Wi-Fi is the only link, so the Pi's chip antenna must keep a clear view out of the enclosure.

- Do not mount the Pi with its antenna end against the panel backs, this board, or the 24 V and
  panel harnesses. Panel PCBs, their ground planes and the board's planes all detune and shadow
  the antenna.
- Keep ≥ 20 mm between the antenna and any copper: this board, panel PCBs, harness bundles.
  Point the antenna toward a PETG wall. PETG is RF-transparent; avoid metal paint, foil or
  carbon-filled filament there.
- The Electrodragon V2 sits on the GPIO header and does not cover the antenna end. Its HUB75
  ribbons should leave from the side away from the antenna.
- Not verified here: the exact antenna position on the Pi 3B. Take it from the Raspberry Pi 3B
  mechanical drawing when placing the Pi in the enclosure model.

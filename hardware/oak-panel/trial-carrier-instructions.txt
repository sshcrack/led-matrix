CARRIER
191.6 x 191.6 x 16 mm. Rear web 4 mm, perimeter rib another 6 mm, four mounting bosses another 12 mm above the web. Four through bores 3.5 mm on the paper-confirmed 174 x 158 mm pattern. The two other 3.5 mm holes are provisional seam-joiner positions for a bottom-left tile, NOT panel mounting holes. Carrier perimeter/body size and 12 mm spacer height remain concept assumptions to be tested.

The +16 mm boss faces touch the panel insert faces. The rear web sits 12 mm behind those faces; the printed rear base is 16 mm behind. Screw heads would remain outside the rear base. This is a mounting/interference trial, not finished carrier-to-frame, wall-mount or fabric-clamp hardware. A complete display must not be hung from this trial part.

COUPON
80 x 24 x 3 mm; from its notched end, holes are 3.2 / 3.4 / 3.5 / 3.6 mm. Record the smallest one that clears the intended fastener freely. These are clearance holes, not printed threads.

PHYSICAL CHECK
1. Inspect flatness and remove loose stringing without changing hole positions.
2. With the panel unplugged, align ONLY the four corner bores. The panels may stay glued together. Boss faces point toward the panel. Do not force the carrier or screws to obtain a fit.
3. Record whether all four boss faces can seat flat, without contact on PCB parts, plastic ribs, glue, HUB75 ribbons or power wiring. The open middle should remain clear. Check intended cable bends in that space and photograph any interference privately.
4. Test the coupon. Confirm a known matching thread before trying screws in panel inserts. A 5.2 mm smooth foot shaft does not identify the thread. Do not use a guessed screw length or tighten into a blind stop. The carrier's 16 mm grip thickness is not the screw length: usable insert engagement and head/washer details are still unknown. Final length will be selected after this check.
5. Mark which edge has HUB75 IN and which carrier edge has the two seam-interface holes. Report alignment, flat seating/interference and coupon clearance. Existing magnetic feet may not have enough exposed stud for the 16 mm grip; do not force them or purchase longer screws by guesswork. Visual fit checking can happen without fastening.

FIRST PRINT ONLY - PETG fit gauges

Submit FIRST_PRINT_mounting_gauge.stl and FIRST_PRINT_hole_coupon.stl together if the service can arrange them as one job. Both flat face down, actual size / 100%, no auto-scaling. Target Prusa i3 MK3s. Ask the service for slicing and price before printing. They may choose suitable print parameters.

The gauge tests the six adopted M3 mounting positions (170 mm between columns, 73 mm row pitch); it is NOT a load-bearing mounting part. Hold gently against the unplugged panel rear; align by holes, not by gauge outer edges.

The coupon tests printed clearance sizes. From its notched end, holes are 3.2 / 3.4 / 3.5 / 3.6 mm. Try actual hardware gently. This does not identify the panel internal thread.

Do not print the full assembly yet. The STL units are millimetres.

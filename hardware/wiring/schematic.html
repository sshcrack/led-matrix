The Mean Well R7B plug drawing shows 2 and 3 (0 V) above 1 and 4 (+24 V) in its keyed view. The Kycon socket drawing uses a different numbering layout. Mate the actual plug and socket and meter-check all four individual cups before joining any wires. With the loads disconnected, each positive cup should read about +24 V relative to each return cup. Unplug the brick before soldering or changing connections. Heat-shrink each cup and recheck polarity at the WAGOs before connecting the modules.

5 · Order for the first power-up

1) Brick → socket only: +24 V at the WAGO, right polarity. 2) Modules A/B without loads: about 5 V at each output. 3) LM2596: set 5.1 V, then connect the Electrodragon + Pi. 4) Panels one cable at a time, dim scene, then bright; feel modules and fuse holders after 10 min. 5) Halo and Pro Micro last.
